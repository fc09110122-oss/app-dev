# Black Clover

## About the Series

**Black Clover** is a fantasy shonen series created by *Yūki Tabata*. The manga began in 2015, and the anime by ***Studio Pierrot*** aired from 2017 to 2021. It follows **Asta**, a boy born without any magic in a world where everyone has it, who dreams of becoming the Wizard King.

> "Surpass your limits! Right now, right here!" A story about never giving up, even without talent.
